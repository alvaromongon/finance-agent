# Portfolio policy (example)

Rules the Monday Briefing respects when it proposes the weekly plan. Free text,
passed to the reasoning model as is.

- Two strict currency silos, EUR and USD; no position is funded by converting
  on the day of the trade.
- At most 25% of a silo in one position; keep enough USD cash for one full
  position.
- No leverage, no derivatives, no short selling.
